DESIGN — 0105 PROJECTS

Visual Identity

Giving ideas an identity.

PROJECT / 01

Re•Born

A second life, by design.

  • Visual identity
  • Sustainability

Re•Born explores how a visual identity can express renewal through simple, memorable forms. A circular flower and sun motif is paired with angular green shapes, connecting the ideas of growth, nature and a fresh beginning. Applied to a growing-kit box, the identity carries that story from the mark into a tangible object. The visual language stays direct and approachable, inviting a closer look at the possibilities of giving everyday materials a second life.

PROJECT / 02

The Blue Shirt

Heritage in a contemporary language.

  • Visual identity
  • Local culture

The Blue Shirt looks to Minnan clothing culture as a starting point for a contemporary identity. A restrained blue palette, carefully constructed Chinese lettering and patterned details bring a sense of place into the system. The stationery applications balance cultural references with generous space and a clear hierarchy. Rather than treating heritage as decoration alone, the project considers how it can become a consistent visual language that feels recognisable, useful and relevant today.

01 / 02

PROJECT / 03

Zao · Tea Rhythm

A quiet conversation with tea.

  • Visual identity
  • Tea culture

Zao · Tea Rhythm brings together the structure of Chinese window frames, the character of traditional lettering and the atmosphere of tea culture. A seal-like mark anchors the identity, while muted brown, red and green tones extend its visual rhythm. The project moves between logo construction and practical applications, seeking a balance between cultural familiarity and contemporary clarity. Its aim is a coherent identity that leaves room for the quiet, considered experience associated with tea.

01 / 02

PROJECT / 04

Twined Flower

A floral language, woven into identity.

  • Visual identity
  • Cultural design

Twined Flower develops a visual identity around Minnan floral references and the movement of intertwining lines. An angular red flower forms the central mark, balanced by flowing blue and yellow details. The pattern system moves between a complete floral composition, cropped motifs and repeating borders, giving the identity different levels of expression. Applied to stationery, cards and envelopes, these elements connect a clear, recognisable symbol with a more intricate graphic language.

PROJECT / 05

Great Tree Pharmacy

Care, rooted in every detail.

  • Visual identity
  • Brand applications

Great Tree Pharmacy explores a caring, approachable visual identity through a tree formed from supporting hands and heart-shaped leaves. Green, warm gold and soft neutral tones connect the symbol to a consistent graphic system. Alternate logo arrangements, repeating motifs and geometric patterns extend the identity across stationery, bags, cups, staff clothing and a membership card. The study focuses on making the same message of care recognisable across everyday touchpoints, from a small printed item to a larger brand application.

PROJECT / 06

Echoes of Bashu

A cultural story, unfolded.

  • Cultural product
  • Pattern design

Echoes of Bashu translates cultural references into a vivid, wearable pattern. Mirrored animal forms, decorative geometry and a symmetrical composition create a visual world that rewards both a distant glance and a closer look. Three colour variations explore different moods while retaining the same underlying structure. By placing these motifs on a silk scarf, the project brings cultural storytelling into an everyday accessory, allowing the pattern to be folded, worn and experienced in more than one way.

PROJECT / 07

The Dopamine Store

A little colour. A little happiness.

  • IP design
  • Creative product

The Dopamine Store is a playful character-led collection built around the simple pleasure of colour. Expressive illustrated personalities appear across keyrings, stationery and small everyday objects, connected by a bright palette and a flexible graphic system. Each application gives the characters a slightly different role while keeping the collection recognisable. The project explores how an approachable visual world can move beyond a single illustration and become a family of objects that brings small moments of delight.

01 / 02

PROJECT / 08

Beijing Culture Bear

One character. Many ways to connect.

  • Character design
  • Cultural products

Beijing Culture Bear builds a character-led visual world around an expressive panda and ornate theatrical headwear. Red, blue and gold details give the character a recognisable silhouette, while a range of expressions and gestures brings flexibility to its personality. The project visualises the character across gift bags, stationery, drinkware, clothing and accessories. Together, the character sheet and product mockups explore how one cultural figure can stay consistent while adapting to different everyday encounters.

Graphic Communication

PROJECT / 09

Break the Rules

Beauty, beyond a fixed definition.

  • Campaign visual
  • Personal Concept

Break the Rules is a personal campaign concept exploring beauty as a form of self-expression. A portrait develops across a sequence of images, with makeup tools and products framing the transformation. The composition uses familiar beauty imagery to question fixed expectations about appearance and identity. Created as an independent visual exploration, the series considers how a social campaign can communicate an idea through progression, contrast and a single, direct message. It is not presented as a commissioned brand campaign.

01 / 08

PROJECT / 10

Messages from the Sea

Public information, made approachable.

  • Poster & flyer
  • Promotional graphics

This collection brings together promotional graphics developed around marine safety and public communication. Posters, banners, printed information and souvenir applications share a maritime vocabulary, combining blue tones, illustration and practical information hierarchy. The challenge is to make important messages accessible across formats with very different viewing distances and uses. Together, the pieces explore how a consistent graphic approach can support both the visibility of an event and a clearer, more approachable encounter with public information.

01 / 02

PROJECT / 11

Mother’s Day Card

A small card, a heartfelt thank you.

  • Greeting card
  • Illustration

This Mother’s Day card turns a simple message of gratitude into a gentle printed keepsake. Pink floral illustrations, an embroidery-hoop motif and a continuous thread-like line connect the cover with the folded interior. A line-drawn hand gesture adds a personal note, while the writing area leaves room for the sender’s own words. The two mockups show how the composition carries across the card’s surfaces, balancing decorative detail with space for a heartfelt message.

DESIGN — 0402 PROJECTS

Packaging

Everyday things, thoughtfully wrapped.

PROJECT / 12

A Daily Brew

A small ritual, bottled.

  • Packaging
  • Coffee

A Daily Brew explores a label system for bottled coffee, treating an everyday drink as a small personal ritual. Individual labels combine typography and illustration, giving each bottle its own expression while keeping the range connected. The clear bottle and dark liquid become part of the composition rather than a surface to conceal. The resulting presentation aims to feel familiar, relaxed and easy to recognise, with enough detail to invite a closer look at the shelf or table.

PROJECT / 13

Flavour, Collected

From ingredient to impression.

  • Packaging
  • Food illustration

Flavour, Collected develops a packaging language for XO sauce through ingredient-led illustration and a warm, restrained palette. Seafood drawings connect the product to its contents, while vertical typography gives the box a distinct rhythm. The jar label and outer packaging share the same visual cues, helping the range read as one family. The project considers how a food package can communicate flavour and character before it is opened, without relying on an overly busy composition.

DESIGN — 0501 PROJECTS

Exhibition & Event

Ideas that become places.

PROJECT / 14

The Coffee Laboratory

An identity you can step into.

  • Exhibition concept
  • Spatial communication

The Coffee Laboratory imagines an exhibition space where coffee can be discovered through display, tasting and conversation. A clear overhead identity connects the booth, while ingredient displays, product shelving and an approachable character create different points of interest. The concept brings visual communication into a spatial setting, considering both recognition from a distance and engagement up close. It is presented as an exhibition design concept, showing how a theme can connect signage, objects and the visitor experience.
